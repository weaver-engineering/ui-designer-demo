# Glossary

## Context
* [Pot Tracker](../PRODUCT.md) - what the product is

Every term this repository's documents use, one line each, linked to where it is defined.

## 1 Terms

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| [All Pots Together](analysis/domain/normalising-growth.md#3-all-pots-together) | The growth index of every pot with a history, as if they were one pot; money moved between the saver's own pots cancels out. |
| [Connected Pot](analysis/domain/pots-and-providers.md#2-how-a-pot-comes-to-pot-tracker) | A pot whose platform shares it with Pot Tracker, kept up to date each day, with holdings and history, while consent lasts. |
| [Consent](analysis/domain/finding-pensions.md#3-consent) | The saver's agreement that a search may be made, or a platform may share, for a time: a year for the search, 90 days for a platform. |
| [General Investment Account](analysis/domain/pots-and-providers.md#1-pots) | An investment account with no tax wrapper and no limit. |
| [Growth Index](analysis/domain/normalising-growth.md#1-the-growth-index) | What one pound in a pot at the start of a period has become, with money moved taken out: every line starts at 1. |
| [Holding](analysis/domain/pots-and-providers.md#1-pots) | One fund or share in a pot of investments: how many units, and their value. |
| [ISA](analysis/domain/pots-and-providers.md#1-pots) | A stocks and shares individual savings account: growth free of tax, paid into up to £20,000 a year. |
| [Money Moved](analysis/domain/pots-and-providers.md#4-money-moved) | A payment in, a withdrawal or a transfer: recorded apart from a pot's value, so never mistaken for growth. |
| [Payment In](analysis/domain/pots-and-providers.md#4-money-moved) | Money into a pot from outside the saver's pots: their bank, or their employer. |
| [Pension-Finding Service](analysis/domain/finding-pensions.md#1-the-pension-finding-service) | The outside service, modelled on the UK's pensions dashboards, that asks every provider for a saver's pensions. |
| [Possible Match](analysis/domain/finding-pensions.md#5-possible-matches) | A pension a provider may hold for the saver but is not sure of; the saver claims it with further proof. |
| [Pot](analysis/domain/pots-and-providers.md#1-pots) | One pension or investment account the saver holds. |
| [Pot Added By Hand](analysis/domain/pots-and-providers.md#2-how-a-pot-comes-to-pot-tracker) | A pot whose value, holdings and history only the saver gives; it cannot be moved from or to. |
| [Projection](analysis/domain/projecting-a-retirement-value.md) | What a pension might be worth at retirement, as a pot and a yearly income, in today's money: an illustration, not a promise. |
| [Promised-Income Pension](analysis/domain/pots-and-providers.md#1-pots) | A workplace pension promising a yearly income from the scheme's retirement age, rather than a pot of money (defined benefit). |
| [Provider](analysis/domain/pots-and-providers.md#3-providers) | Who holds a pot: a pension scheme, an insurer or an investment platform. |
| [Saver](analysis/user-personas/mid-career-saver.md) | Anyone using Pot Tracker; each of the three personas is one. The actor of every use case. |
| [Today's Money](analysis/domain/projecting-a-retirement-value.md#3-working-it-out) | A future amount brought back by assumed inflation, so it reads as what it would buy now. |
| [Transfer](analysis/domain/pots-and-providers.md#4-money-moved) | Money moved from one of the saver's pots to another, carried out by the providers, through stages. |
| [Transfer Stage](analysis/domain/pots-and-providers.md#6-a-transfers-stages) | Where a transfer has got to: asked for, agreed, money moving, done; or cancelled, or refused. |
| [What A Move Has Been Worth](analysis/domain/normalising-growth.md#7-what-a-move-has-been-worth) | Money moved, grown by where it went, as a percentage of where it came from or of where else it could have gone. |
| [Withdrawal](analysis/domain/pots-and-providers.md#4-money-moved) | Money out of a pot to the saver's bank. |
| [Yearly Rate](analysis/domain/normalising-growth.md#5-the-yearly-rate) | Growth over a period as the rate a year that would give it, so periods of different lengths compare. |
