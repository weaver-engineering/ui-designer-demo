# The Mid-Career Saver Persona

A saver in the middle of their working life, with pensions left behind at every job they have had.

## Context
* [Pot Tracker](../../../PRODUCT.md) - the root index for this repo
* [Near-Retirement Planner](near-retirement-planner.md) and [Hands-On Investor](hands-on-investor.md) - the other
  savers Pot Tracker serves

## 1 Role

A saver: the primary actor of every use case, as the other personas are. The use cases they come to Pot Tracker for:

* **Getting in and out:** [sign-up](../use-cases/sign-up/USE-CASE.md), [sign-in](../use-cases/sign-in/USE-CASE.md),
  [sign-out](../use-cases/sign-out/USE-CASE.md).
* **Finding what they have:** [find-my-pensions](../use-cases/find-my-pensions/USE-CASE.md), the reason they signed up,
  and [claim-a-possible-match](../use-cases/claim-a-possible-match/USE-CASE.md) for the pensions a provider is not
  sure are theirs.
* **Understanding it:** [see-what-a-pension-is-worth](../use-cases/see-what-a-pension-is-worth/USE-CASE.md).
* **Tidying it up, one day:** [transfer-between-pots](../use-cases/transfer-between-pots/USE-CASE.md).

## 2 Goals

1. Know every pension they have, and where it is.
2. Know roughly what it all adds up to.
3. Decide, eventually, whether to bring some pots together.

## 3 Frustrations

1. Losing track of a workplace pension when they changed jobs, and not knowing who to ask.
2. Letters from providers they don't recognise, each showing one pot and none of the others.
3. Every provider having its own website, sign-in and way of showing a value.
4. Not knowing whether a small old pot is worth the trouble of moving.
5. Being asked for a policy number they never had.

## 4 Financial and Technical Proficiency

Moderate with technology, low with pensions. Comfortable with a banking app on their phone, which is where they will
mostly use Pot Tracker. They know what a pension is, but not the difference between a defined contribution and a
defined benefit scheme, and shouldn't need to: what they are shown is in plain words, a value in pounds, and a provider's
name, never a scheme reference where a name exists.
