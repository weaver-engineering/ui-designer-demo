# The Near-Retirement Planner Persona

A saver within ten years or so of retiring, who wants to know whether they will have enough.

## Context
* [Pot Tracker](../../../PRODUCT.md) - the root index for this repo
* [Mid-Career Saver](mid-career-saver.md) and [Hands-On Investor](hands-on-investor.md) - the other savers Pot Tracker
  serves

## 1 Role

A saver: the primary actor of every use case, as the other personas are. The use cases they come to Pot Tracker for:

* **Getting in and out:** [sign-up](../use-cases/sign-up/USE-CASE.md), [sign-in](../use-cases/sign-in/USE-CASE.md),
  [sign-out](../use-cases/sign-out/USE-CASE.md).
* **Seeing the whole picture:** [find-my-pensions](../use-cases/find-my-pensions/USE-CASE.md),
  [see-what-a-pension-is-worth](../use-cases/see-what-a-pension-is-worth/USE-CASE.md), the reason they signed up.
* **Bringing it together:** [transfer-between-pots](../use-cases/transfer-between-pots/USE-CASE.md).

## 2 Goals

1. Know what their pensions might give them at the age they mean to retire.
2. See how a different retirement age, or paying in more, changes that.
3. Bring their pots together into fewer, and follow each transfer until it is done.

## 3 Frustrations

1. Each provider projecting on its own assumptions, so the numbers don't add up to anything.
2. Not knowing whether a projection is in today's money or tomorrow's.
3. Transfers taking weeks, with no word of where one has got to.
4. Money being out of the market while a transfer is under way, without being told.
5. Being worried by a single bad year without seeing the longer trend.

## 4 Financial and Technical Proficiency

Moderate with pensions, moderate to low with technology. They have read their annual statements, and know a defined
benefit pension from a defined contribution one. They use Pot Tracker mostly on a laptop or a tablet, and read
carefully. A projection is always shown with what it assumes, and as an illustration, never as a promise.
