# The Hands-On Investor Persona

A saver who chooses their own investments, in a self-invested pension and an ISA, and cares how they perform.

## Context
* [Pot Tracker](../../../PRODUCT.md) - the root index for this repo
* [Mid-Career Saver](mid-career-saver.md) and [Near-Retirement Planner](near-retirement-planner.md) - the other savers
  Pot Tracker serves

## 1 Role

A saver: the primary actor of every use case, as the other personas are. The use cases they come to Pot Tracker for:

* **Getting in and out:** [sign-up](../use-cases/sign-up/USE-CASE.md), [sign-in](../use-cases/sign-in/USE-CASE.md),
  [sign-out](../use-cases/sign-out/USE-CASE.md).
* **Bringing everything in:** [add-an-investment-portfolio](../use-cases/add-an-investment-portfolio/USE-CASE.md),
  [find-my-pensions](../use-cases/find-my-pensions/USE-CASE.md).
* **Judging performance:** [compare-growth](../use-cases/compare-growth/USE-CASE.md), the reason they signed up.
* **Moving money:** [transfer-between-pots](../use-cases/transfer-between-pots/USE-CASE.md).

## 2 Goals

1. See every pot they own, pension or not, in one place.
2. Compare how each has really grown, without the money they moved in and out flattering or hiding it.
3. Move money to where it does best, and see the effect.

## 3 Frustrations

1. A provider's "gain" counting their own contributions as growth.
2. Not being able to compare a pension and an ISA on the same terms.
3. Each platform's charts starting at different dates and measuring different things.
4. Re-typing holdings by hand whenever they want a combined view.

## 4 Financial and Technical Proficiency

High with both. They know what a time-weighted return is, and expect to be able to choose the period they compare
over. They use Pot Tracker on a large screen when analysing, and on their phone to check in. They still want plain
labels: a percentage and a period, not a formula.
