Find my pensions

Pot Tracker can find the pensions you have, including ones from old jobs you've lost track of.

Who is searched
Every UK pension provider, through the pension-finding service.
What is shared
You prove who you are to the pension-finding service, not to us. Each provider that finds a pension shares its name, the employer it came from, what kind it is and its value with Pot Tracker. Nothing else.
How long it takes
Providers answer in their own time: most within a day, some up to a week. You don't need to wait here; we'll tell you what they find.
